### Time series controls

  1. `Calculate for coordinates`: Check this on to calculate time series for especific coordinates.  

  1. `Select a region`: Select a region to obtain and visualize the time series. You can use the search box to look for an specific region (all provinces, counties and municipalities are available).  

  1. `Longitude`and `Latitude`: When the coordinates check is on, longitude and latitude can be supplied. Max precision is 3 decimal points. As the coordinates time series calculation is expensive, once the coordinates have been introduced, the `Calculate time series` button must be pressed.  

  1. Dates are limited to the last 365 days since the most recent date available.  
    - The most recent date available is usually 5 days ago from the actual date, due to limitations imposed by the data sources (meteorological daily data is only available after 5 days).

### Time series exploration

Using the mouse you can modify the time series view. All time series are linked, so any change on any of them will affect all the plots.

  - Hovering over the time series will show the dates and the values for that date.
  - Scrolling will increase/reduce the time series zoom.

### Time series download

Visualized timeseries can be downloaded in text format (`csv`) by pressing the
`Download csv` button.
